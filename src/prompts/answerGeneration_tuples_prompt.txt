

Structured Facts:
- The evidence may end with structured facts: "entity1 relation entity2 (conditions: c1; c2; ...)". The conditions say for which patients the fact holds.
- Use the facts to decide which answer is correct for THIS patient:
  1. Identify the patient-specific conditions in the question (e.g. pregnancy, allergies, comorbidities, age, organ function, co-medication).
  2. Compare each relevant fact's conditions with the patient: a fact applies if its conditions match the patient; it does not apply if its conditions contradict the patient.
  3. Rule out options that an applicable fact marks as contraindicated, avoided or not recommended for a condition the patient has.
  4. Among the remaining options, choose the one supported by the fact whose conditions match the patient most specifically.
- A condition not mentioned in the question is unknown, not contradicted: the fact may still apply, but prefer facts whose conditions are explicitly met.
- If the text passages suggest a general answer but an applicable fact shows that the patient's conditions require a different one, follow the fact; use the text passages for context and to confirm the answer.
- Still give a single best answer, never a list of options
